# Pilot 02 Visual Review

## Review protocol

The initial three normalized candidates for every slide and the later user-requested divergence passes were inspected together at `1920×1080` and again through the `1280×720` previews. Visible copy was compared character-by-character with `manifest.yaml`; dimensions, RGB mode, clipping, contrast, reading order, expression level, semantic geometry, and family consistency were reviewed before promotion. Normalization was resize-only. Selected draft and canonical hashes are identical.

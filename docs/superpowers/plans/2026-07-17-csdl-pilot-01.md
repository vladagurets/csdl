# CSDL Pilot 01 — 16:9 Restart Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Produce and validate the first publish-ready Constructive Signal presentation series: seven canonical `1920×1080` light-mode slides about agentic discipline, one contact sheet, seven landscape review previews, a reusable Prompt DSL packet, and a scored evaluation record.

**Restart decision:** Previous 4:5 Pilot 01 rasters, prompts, approvals, scores, mobile previews, and adaptation tasks are superseded. Git history remains the archive. Canonical copy and the locked CSDL direction do not change.

## Global contract

- Canonical canvas: exactly `1920×1080`, 16:9, landscape.
- No portrait master and no mobile-preview deliverable.
- Seven-slide rhythm: A → A → B → A → B → A → C.
- One main idea, one visual mechanism, and one dominant signal per slide.
- Generate three independent candidates for the shared anchor and for every slide.
- D-036 overlay for every new or regenerated pass on or after 2026-09-03: `v1`, `v2`, and `v3` preserve one approved content contract but use separate prompts and differ materially in conceptual framing, dominant visual mechanism, and composition topology. Do not treat mirroring, repositioning, palette/shape/scale/spacing changes, or decorative substitutions as separate directions.
- Exact text from `manifest.yaml`; no added labels, logos, footers, or UI chrome.
- Candidate acceptance: clarity, presentation readability, and text fidelity = 5; every other criterion ≥ 4; average ≥ 4.4.
- Drafts live under ignored `drafts/light/16x9/`; only approved outputs live under `canonical/light/16x9/`.

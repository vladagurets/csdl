**Date:** 2026-07-18

**Scope:** generated Milestone 2 families 02–12 and 14; Pilot-backed Hero, Comparison, and Loop remain accepted.

## Finding

The first Milestone 2 generation pass satisfied exact-copy, semantic, raster, and per-slide readability checks but failed the series-level CSDL identity requirement. The contact sheet collapsed toward generic centered infographics: wide neutral display type, thin-line mechanisms, similar density across families, weak Anchor/Plane relationships, and insufficient Level A/B/C differentiation.

Root cause: every Prompt DSL and review used only `pilots/01-agentic-discipline/references/style-anchor-light.png`. That slide is valid secondary evidence for Ukrainian text, landscape framing, paper tone, and Quiet spacing, but it cannot carry the complete language shown by the three boards in `references/canonical/`.

## Authority correction

The three canonical boards are now primary visual authority for display typography, components, semantic geometry, asymmetry, contrast, and expression-level identity. The Pilot landscape anchor is secondary execution evidence. All four references must be attached and role-labeled in every new built-in GPT Image 2 call.

# 03 Quote

## Problem

Give one exact statement enough visual authority to be read as an idea, not as decorative typography.

## Allowed scenarios

Principle, short testimony, or deliberate pause between dense sections.

## Semantic components

The quotation is the `Anchor`; attribution is subordinate; one small `Signal` marks the hinge or source relation.

## Assembly order

1. Set the exact quotation as a readable block.
2. Isolate the attribution below or beside it.
3. Add one small semantic Signal.

## Expression levels

Levels A and B are allowed. A is canonical. C is excluded because long display copy would become poster-like and reduce readability.

## Typography and spacing

Use `display.h1` or large `body.lead`, never condensed body text; preserve at least 65% negative space and one horizontal reading direction.

## Signal constraints

Coral occupies no more than 6% and may mark the opening quotation edge, not each line.

## Canonical content

The quotation and `AGENTIC DISCIPLINE` attribution in the manifest, including punctuation.

## Hard exclusions

No giant decorative quotation marks, portrait silhouette, fabricated speaker, rotated quote, or extra source metadata.

## Prompt DSL contract

`prompts/03-quote.yaml` defines exact Ukrainian Unicode and a quiet left-to-right composition.

## Acceptance criteria

Exact quote and attribution; natural reading at `1280×720`; one typographic gesture at most; no invented speaker.

## Canonical evidence

One new canonical example selected from three built-in GPT Image 2 candidates.

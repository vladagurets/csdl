# 02 Cover

## Problem

Name a series or section while establishing one visual premise rather than previewing all content.

## Allowed scenarios

Series opening or chapter divider. Not a dense agenda or promotional poster.

## Semantic components

The title is the `Anchor`, the open `Field` establishes scope, and one `Signal` plane creates the entry moment.

## Assembly order

1. Place the short title Anchor.
2. Preserve an open Field around it.
3. Add one Signal plane that directs the reading path without covering text.

## Expression levels

Level A is allowed for quiet section dividers. Level C is canonical for the single opening peak. Level B adds no distinct semantic value and is excluded.

## Typography and spacing

Use `display.hero` with a compact technical subtitle, 96/72 px safe margins, and at least 55% perceived negative space despite the Level C plane.

## Signal constraints

One coral plane may occupy 12–22% of the canvas. It must establish scope or direction, not decorate a corner.

## Canonical content

`СИСТЕМИ, ЩО ВЧАТЬСЯ` and `VISUAL DNA SPRINT 1`, exactly.

## Hard exclusions

No agenda, logo, date, author credit, mock interface, multiple Signal planes, or historic-poster treatment.

## Prompt DSL contract

`prompts/02-cover.yaml` defines a direct landscape Level C cover using the approved style anchor.

## Acceptance criteria

Exact two text blocks; only one Level C peak; strong title hierarchy; no illegible overlap; publishable at `1280×720`.

## Canonical evidence

One new canonical example selected from three built-in GPT Image 2 candidates.

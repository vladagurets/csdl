# 06 Collision

## Problem

Show two forces producing one consequential intersection, constraint, or synthesis.

## Allowed scenarios

Constraint, synthesis, tension, or trade-off where the overlap has a named meaning.

## Semantic components

Two `Anchor`s meet in one `Collision`; the overlap is the `Signal`, not an explosion effect.

## Assembly order

1. Place two opposing Anchors with clear identities.
2. Create one controlled overlap.
3. Label the result at the overlap.

## Expression levels

Levels B and C are allowed because interaction is the mechanism. B is canonical. A would hide the necessary force; C is reserved for a culmination.

## Typography and spacing

Keep all labels horizontal, use balanced display scale, and retain at least 50% negative space around the interaction.

## Signal constraints

Coral marks only the intersection and occupies no more than 12%.

## Canonical content

`ШВИДКІСТЬ`, `КОНТРОЛЬ`, and `НАДІЙНІСТЬ`, exactly.

## Hard exclusions

No debris, sparks, impact rays, decorative shards, boxing-match `VS`, or unlabeled overlap.

## Prompt DSL contract

`prompts/06-collision.yaml` defines a Level B synthesis with one readable result.

## Acceptance criteria

The two inputs and one result are unambiguous; overlap explains synthesis; no decorative violence or extra text.

## Canonical evidence

One new canonical example selected from three built-in GPT Image 2 candidates.

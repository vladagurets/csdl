# 01 Hero

## Problem

Make one proposition understandable in 2–3 seconds and memorable after the slide is gone.

## Allowed scenarios

Opening thesis, section thesis, or key takeaway with one dominant claim.

## Semantic components

One `Anchor` carries the proposition. One `Signal` supplies the counterweight or changed state. Supporting copy explains, never competes.

## Assembly order

1. Place the Anchor across four–seven columns.
2. Isolate one supporting sentence.
3. Add one functional Signal on the reading path.

## Expression levels

Levels A, B, and C are allowed. A is canonical; B adds one functional Vector; C is reserved for a very short culmination.

## Typography and spacing

Use `display.hero` or `display.h1`, neutral body copy, 96 px horizontal margins, and 65–75% negative space at Level A.

## Signal constraints

The coral Signal is a stable counterweight and occupies no more than 8% of the canvas.

## Canonical content

Exact Pilot 01 headline and supporting sentence from the catalog manifest.

## Hard exclusions

No decorative geometry, extra labels, poster texture, rotated body copy, logo, footer, or UI chrome.

## Prompt DSL contract

`prompts/01-hero.yaml` formalizes the Pilot 01 Hero composition and points to the original generation prompt.

## Acceptance criteria

Exact copy; clear Anchor → Signal route; Level A restraint; natural `1280×720` readability; Pilot score threshold met.

## Canonical evidence

Pilot 01 Card 01 Hook, its Prompt DSL, three-candidate review, accepted score, and pinned SHA-256.

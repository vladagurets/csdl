# 05 Comparison

## Problem

Compare two systems clearly without implying adversarial conflict unless conflict is explicitly the content.

## Allowed scenarios

Approach comparison, complementary roles, or a bounded trade-off with at most three criteria per side.

## Semantic components

Two open `Field`s hold equal `Anchor`s, one `Divider` preserves distinction, and one small `Signal` marks the shared comparison.

## Assembly order

1. Establish equal open Fields.
2. Align titles and three criteria.
3. Separate with one thin Divider.
4. Add one shared Signal.

## Expression levels

A, B, and C are allowed. A is canonical. B requires a functional Collision; C is reserved for principled conflict.

## Typography and spacing

Use equal title roles, neutral readable criteria, 96 px margins, and generous central separation.

## Signal constraints

The coral Signal is shared and occupies no more than 5%. Repeated colored bullets are forbidden.

## Canonical content

Exact Pilot 01 Card 04 headline, titles, six criteria, and supporting sentence.

## Hard exclusions

No decorative `VS`, shards, unequal moral framing, repeated colored bullets, extra criteria, or dashboard cards.

## Prompt DSL contract

`prompts/05-comparison.yaml` formalizes the Pilot 01 complementary comparison and cites the source prompt.

## Acceptance criteria

All ten text blocks exact; equal hierarchy; complementary rather than adversarial reading; Pilot score threshold met.

## Canonical evidence

Pilot 01 Card 04 Comparison, its Prompt DSL, three-candidate review, accepted score, and pinned SHA-256.

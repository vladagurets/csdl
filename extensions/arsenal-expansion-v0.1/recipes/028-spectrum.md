# Recipe 028: Spectrum

## Problem

Position concepts on one named continuum without inventing a second dimension.

## Distinction

`Matrix` requires two independent dimensions. `Spectrum` requires one ordered
semantic `Axis` with explicit endpoints and optional bounded zones.

## Ingredients

One `Axis`, two to six `Node` positions, one to three `Band` zones, one
`Signal`, and optional `Threshold` conditions.

## Acceptance

Endpoints name the continuum. Position has declared meaning. The slide does not
imply precision beyond the supplied content.

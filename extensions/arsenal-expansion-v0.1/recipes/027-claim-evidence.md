# Recipe 027: Claim / Evidence

## Problem

Make the support for a proposition visible and keep the verdict separate from
the evidence itself.

## Distinction

`Checklist` owns readiness questions. `Claim / Evidence` owns argument support,
confidence, limitations, and a verdict.

## Ingredients

One claim `Anchor`, one evidence `Cluster`, two to five evidence `Node`
instances, one `Trace`, and one verdict `Signal`.

## Acceptance

Evidence supports rather than causes the claim. Missing evidence and limitations
remain visible. The verdict cannot exceed the declared support.

# Recipe 029: Anatomy

## Problem

Explain the functional parts of one subject while preserving it as one whole.

## Distinction

`Architecture` owns system boundaries and interactions. `Hierarchy` owns
decomposition or authority. `Anatomy` owns named functional parts of one subject.

## Ingredients

One subject `Anchor`, one `Cluster`, three to six part `Node` instances, direct
`Label` instances, and an optional `Signal`.

## Acceptance

Every part names a function. No part becomes a separate system or chronological
stage. Connectors are omitted unless topology is necessary to interpretation.

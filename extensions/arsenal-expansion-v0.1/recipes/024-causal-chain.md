# Recipe 024: Causal Chain

## Problem

Explain cause, mechanism, and consequence without presenting mere chronology as causation.

## Distinction

`Workflow` owns operational order. `Causal Chain` owns an explicit causal claim
and therefore requires the mechanism between cause and consequence.

## Ingredients

Three to five `Node` instances, two to four `Vector` instances, one `Signal`,
and optional direct `Label` instances.

## Acceptance

The cause, mechanism, and consequence are all named; the decisive causal link
is visible once; no unsubstantiated branch or decorative arrow is added.

# Band

## Meaning

`Band` is a bounded interval on a declared `Axis`. It may encode tolerance,
uncertainty, a time window, or a semantic zone. It is not a background panel.

## Uses

Spectrum zones, roadmap windows, KPI tolerance, forecast intervals, and range
comparisons.

## Exclusions

Generic background fills, unconstrained gradients, decorative stripes, and
intervals without explicit lower and upper bounds.

# Trace

## Meaning

`Trace` is a route that was observed, executed, or supported by evidence.
`Vector` communicates direction or action; `Bridge` communicates topology;
neither claims that traversal occurred.

## Uses

Execution history, critical path, evidence chain, observed state transition,
and provenance route.

## Exclusions

Decorative connectors, hypothetical arrows, continuous progress, and any route
without a declared evidence status.
